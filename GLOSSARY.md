# Alamir website

## Forms

**Form purpose**: The visitor task a form supports: home enquiry, contact, quote, application, reservation, search, or a custom page form.

**Form owner**: The page, product, job, or event to which a managed form belongs. A record-specific form belongs to that record rather than to a general page.

**Form scope**: A form purpose together with its owner collection and, when specified, its owner. A scope without a specific owner can identify the general page forms for that purpose.

## Content

**Published content**: Content approved for the public website. Draft content is separate and available only through authenticated preview.
